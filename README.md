# Customer Satisfaction & Call Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end interactive dashboard analyzing customer satisfaction metrics, call volumes, channel performance, and SLA compliance. This project extracts key operational insights to highlight opportunities for improving customer experience and service efficiency.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary

* **Customer Satisfaction:** Stands at **5.55 / 10** average CSAT, driven down by a **51.9% negative sentiment rate**.
* **SLA Compliance:** **62.6%** of interactions were handled within SLA, while **13% breached SLA**.
* **Primary Drivers:** Overwhelming call volume stems from **Billing Questions (23,462 calls)** compared to Payments (4,749) and Service Outages (4,730).

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics Overview

* **Total Calls Received:** 32.94K
* **Rated Calls:** 12K (Response Rate: 37.3%)
* **Average CSAT:** 5.55 / 10
* **Average Handle Time (AHT):** 25.02 minutes
* **Within SLA:** 62.6% (20,625 calls)
* **Below SLA / Breached:** 13% SLA Breach (4,168 calls Above SLA target, 8,148 Below SLA)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Insights

1. **Channel Breakdown:**
   * **Call-Center:** 32.3%
   * **Chatbot:** 25.06%
   * **Email:** 22.68%
   * **Web:** 19.96%

2. **Call Topics & Volume Distribution:**
   * High concentration of inquiries related to **Billing Questions** (~71% of total categorized calls).
   * Operational focus required on billing transparency to reduce overall support ticket load.

3. **Geographic Analysis:**
   * Geographic heat map tracking call volume distribution across US states, with heavy activity centered in major states like Texas and California.

4. **Daily Volume Trends:**
   * Tracks day-of-month fluctuation patterns ranging between ~1,040 to 1,170 calls per day.
